Research interests

Plasma assisted processes

  • Numerical simulation of plasma processes
    • simulation of the first stages of RF-CCP discharges

  • Plasma assisted surface modification of materials at the nanoscale
    • plasma-assisted surface modification of Ti surfaces for biomedical applications
    • low temperature, plasma-assisted (low pressure and APP) surface modification of carbon fibers and textiles

  • Non-thermal plasma assisted abatement processes
    • plasma-driven catalysis (PDC) processes for the abatement of atmospheric pollutants

Materials

  • Amorphous and nanocrystalline materials
    • amorphous Si-based materials (a-Si:H, a-SiCx:H, a-SiNx:H, a-SiOx, a-SiOxNy, a-SiCxOyNz)
    • Si nanocrystals embedded in a-SiCx, a-SiNx or a-SiOx matrix
    • amorphous C-based materials (a-C:H, a-CNx:H)
    • a-WO3 electrochromic thin-films
    • ITO thin films on glass and polumeric substrates
    • Cr nanolayers on polymeric substrates

  • Mono/polycrystalline materials
    • microcrystalline and polycrystalline Si
    • polycrystalline and epitaxial SiC

  • Multilayer structures
    • Si-based multilayer structures for optical filters and resonant cavities

  • Biomedical applications
    • Bioactive coatings for osseointegration acceleration
    • Antibacterial coatings
    • Coatings for biocompatibility improvement
    • Barrier coatings against diffusion of metal ions

Devices

  • PV and energy saving applications
    • thin-film, Si-based transparent solar cells
    • electrochromic devices

  • Sensing applications
    • a-Si:H and a-SiC:H based UV detectors

  • Power electronics applications
    • Schottky diodes based on 4H-SiC

  • Biomedical applications
    • cell adhesion sensors
    • surface modified dental implants
